Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Calculator - Information

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed sum in a mutual fund scheme on a regular basis. It allows investors to invest small amounts at regular intervals instead of making a lump-sum investment. SIPs help in achieving financial goals by investing consistently over time and benefiting from the power of compounding.

How SIP Works?

SIP works by allowing you to invest a fixed amount at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly, etc.) into a mutual fund scheme. The amount gets automatically deducted from your bank account and invested in a fund. Depending on the NAV (Net Asset Value) of the fund at that time, you purchase units. Over time, this builds up your investment portfolio.

Benefits of SIP

  • Discipline in Investing: SIP encourages regular investing, which helps in building wealth over time.
  • Rupee Cost Averaging: When you invest consistently, you buy more units when the prices are low and fewer when the prices are high, thus averaging the cost of investment.
  • Power of Compounding: Even small investments grow over time due to compound interest, making SIP an effective way to achieve long-term financial goals.
  • Flexibility: You can start SIPs with as low as ₹500 and increase the investment amount as per your convenience.
  • Goal-Oriented Savings: SIPs help in saving and investing systematically towards your financial goals like buying a house, children's education, or retirement.

Why Use a SIP Calculator?

A SIP calculator helps in estimating the future value of your investments based on the monthly investment amount, tenure, and expected rate of return. It is a useful tool to plan your investments and achieve your financial goals more efficiently. By entering the SIP details, you can get an idea of how much wealth you can accumulate over time.

SIP Formula

The formula to calculate the future value of a SIP investment is as follows:

Future Value (FV) = P × [(1 + r)^n - 1] / r

Where:

  • FV: Future Value of the investment
  • P: Amount invested in each period
  • r: Rate of interest per period (monthly rate of return)
  • n: Total number of periods (months)

Key Factors to Consider:

  • Investment Amount: The amount you invest every month.
  • Tenure: The time period for which you will invest regularly.
  • Expected Rate of Return: The estimated annual return you expect from the mutual fund.
